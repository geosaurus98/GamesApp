Swipe to slide the tiles. Matching numbers merge. Reach 2048!

How to play

  • Swipe up, down, left or right (or use the arrow keys) to slide every tile.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into one: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8…
  • After every move a new 2 or 4 appears.
  • Make a 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score.
  • The game ends when the board is full and nothing can merge. Undo takes back your last move.
