Bananagrams

How to play
  • Build a crossword grid using every tile in your rack. Words read left‑to‑right or top‑to‑bottom and must all connect.
  • Drag tiles onto the board, or tap a tile then tap a square. Drag a board tile back to the rack to take it back.
  • When your rack is empty and every word is valid, hit Peel! to draw one more tile.
  • Stuck on a letter? Drag it onto Dump (or select it and tap Dump) to swap it for three new tiles.
  • When the bunch is empty and your grid is complete, call Bananas! to win.
  • Pinch or use + / − to zoom. Drag empty space to move around.
