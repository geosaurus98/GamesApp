Blackjack

How to play
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their number, J/Q/K count 10, and an Ace is 1 or 11.
  • Tap chips to bet, then Deal. Hit takes a card, Stand stops.
  • Double: double your bet and take exactly one more card. Split: turn a pair into two hands (you put in another bet).
  • The dealer must hit until 17. A blackjack (Ace + 10-card) pays 3 to 2.
