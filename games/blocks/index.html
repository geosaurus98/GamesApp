How to play
  • Fit the falling blocks together. A full row disappears and scores points; clearing several rows at once scores much more.
  • On the board: tap to rotate, drag left or right to move, drag down to drop faster, flick down to drop straight away, flick up to hold.
  • Buttons: ⇄ hold, ◀ ▶ move, ⟳ rotate, ▼ faster, ⤓ drop.
  • Hold keeps a piece for later (once per piece). The faint outline shows where the piece will land.
  • Every 10 rows the level goes up and blocks fall faster.
