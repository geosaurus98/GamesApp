Crazy Eights

How to play
  • Be the first to get rid of all your cards.
  • Play a card that matches the top card's suit or number. Playable cards are lifted up.
  • Eights are wild: play one any time and choose the next suit.
  • Can't play? Tap the deck to draw (up to 3 cards), then Pass if you still can't.
  • The winner scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: eights 50, pictures 10, others their number.
