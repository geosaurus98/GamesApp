FreeCell

How to play
  • Move every card up to the four piles at the top right, one per suit, Ace to King.
  • In the columns, stack cards downward in alternating colours.
  • The four spaces at the top left each hold any one card while you work.
  • You can move several cards at once if there are enough free spaces to shuffle them through.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it. Cards that are safe to go home go up by themselves.
  • Almost every deal can be won. Deals are numbered like the classic game, so you can replay or share one.
