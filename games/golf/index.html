Golf

How to play
  • Clear the seven columns by moving their bottom cards onto the pile.
  • A card can go on the pile if it's one higher or one lower than the top card, whatever the suit. By default Kings and Aces connect too; you can switch that off in this menu for the traditional rules.
  • Tap a card to play it (or drag it to the pile). Tap the deck when you're stuck to turn over a new pile card.
  • Like real golf, a low score wins: it's the cards left on the table. Clear them all and every unused deck card counts as a stroke under.
