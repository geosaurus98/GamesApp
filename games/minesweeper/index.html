Minesweeper

How to play
  • Clear every square that doesn't hide a mine.
  • Tap to dig. Your first tap is always safe.
  • A number tells you how many mines touch that square (including diagonally).
  • Long-press (or switch to 🚩 Flag mode) to mark a square you think is a mine.
  • Tap a number that already has the right number of flags around it to dig all its other neighbours at once.
