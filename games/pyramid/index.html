Pyramid

How to play
  • Clear the pyramid by removing pairs of cards that add up to 13. Jacks are 11, Queens 12, and Kings (13) go on their own.
  • Only cards with nothing resting on them can be used.
  • Tap two cards to pair them (or drag one onto the other). Tap a King to remove it.
  • Tap the deck to turn over a card; the top face-up card can be paired too. You can go through the deck three times.
