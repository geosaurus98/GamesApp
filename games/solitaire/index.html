Solitaire

How to play
  • Move every card up to the four piles at the top right, one per suit, from Ace to King.
  • In the seven columns, stack cards downward in alternating colours (red 7 on a black 8). Only a King can go in an empty column.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it (with the cards on top of it) where you want.
  • Tap the deck at the top left to turn over new cards. When it runs out, tap again to go through it once more.
  • Stuck? Try Hint or Undo.
