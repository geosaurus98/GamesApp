Spelling Hive

How to play
  • Make words with the letters in the hive. Every word must use the centre letter.
  • Words need at least 4 letters. Letters can be used more than once.
  • 4-letter words score 1 point; longer words score 1 point per letter.
  • A pangram uses all seven letters and scores 7 extra points.
  • Real words that aren't on the puzzle's list still count as ★ bonus words.
