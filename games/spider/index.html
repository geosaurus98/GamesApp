How to play
  • Build a run from King down to Ace in one suit. Finished runs leave the table. Clear all eight to win.
  • Any card can go on a card one higher, whatever the suit, but only runs of the same suit can be moved together.
  • Tap a card to move it (and the cards on it) to the best spot, or drag it.
  • Stuck? Deal puts one new card on every column. You can't deal while a column is empty.
