How to play
  • Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 once each.
  • Tap a square, then tap a number.
  • Turn on Notes to pencil in possible numbers. They clear themselves when you place a number nearby.
  • Hint fills in the selected square (or a random one).
