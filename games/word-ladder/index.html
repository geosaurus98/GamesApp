How to play

Climb from the top word to the bottom word, changing one letter at a time. Every step must be a real word.

COLD → CORD → CARD → WARD → WARM

  • Tap a letter in your word, then tap a new letter on the keyboard. Press Enter to add the word.
  • Green letters already match the goal word.
  • Par is how many steps the puzzle can be done in using everyday words. Beat it if you can!
  • Stuck? Hint shows a next step. Undo takes a step back.
